## What's new in v1.2.0

Since v1.1.1:

- **Clean course document.** A readable copy of the course without the Blueprint markup: a contents list that jumps to each activity, the real due dates, and optional page breaks. It saves as a Google Doc or an .html file.
- **Choose from my courses.** Pick a course from a searchable list instead of pasting an address. Each course shows your role by its Canvas name, such as Teacher, Designer or Course Administrator. The Validate button now shows clearly whether the course has been checked.
- **Dropdown smart chips in the Google Doc.** In the Course content document, the Canvas tool name on each tool line (Page, Assignment, Quiz…) and each Blueprint icon label on a heading ([List Icon], [Video Icon]…) is a dropdown, like in the Blueprint template. The black dot after tool names is gone: a Page line is just the dropdown, and assignment, discussion and quiz lines read "…; Link to settings tab".
- **Videos and broken links.** Embedded YouTube, Panopto and Vimeo videos used to disappear from Google Docs. Now each one shows up with a link to the video, using the video's own title: as a small video card in the Clean course document, and as an "Embed video:" line in the Course content document. Links that cannot work are highlighted in light red, and so are YouTube and Vimeo videos that have been deleted. To find those, the app asks YouTube and Vimeo whether a video still exists and sends only the video's public address.
- **More ways to pick content.** In the Course content and Clean course document pickers, each module has an **Everything in this module** checkbox that selects or clears all of its items at once. You can also choose the home page and syllabus on their own, and a final group lists published content that is not in any module.
- **Clearer messages, safer handling.** Plain-English error messages, a clearer Google sign-in with a Cancel button, and a prompt before your Canvas token is sent to a site other than Boise State's.

Also in this version: document titles show the course address and extraction time, saved files are named after the course and open in your Downloads folder, and several smaller fixes.
